SPECTRA
=======

**S**\ calable **P**\ latform for **E**\ xtracting **C**\ onstraint-based
**T**\ op-down **R**\ econstructions and **A**\ nalysis.

SPECTRA checks flux consistency and reconstructs metabolic networks from
multi-omics data at a range of biological scales. What changes between a
context-specific model, a gap-filled draft and a minimal reactome is the
universal model, the evidence supplied and the objective chosen, not the
routine called.

The scripts, models and data behind the manuscript live on the
`paper_codes <https://github.com/bisect-group/SPECTRA/tree/paper_codes>`_
branch. SPECTRA is also available as a Python package for cobrapy,
`spectra-cobra <https://github.com/bisect-group/spectra-cobra>`_.
